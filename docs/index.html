Milán Kas (b. 2002) is an architect student based in Budapest, Hungary. He is expected to complete his Bachelor's degree in Architecture in 2026 at the Moholy-Nagy University of Art and Design, Budapest. He has worked at Paradigma Ariadné as an intern, helping their material research and modeling. His talents have been recognised internationally with a variety of honors, such as Stefan Lengyel Scholarship of Excellence and JAFHolz CLT idea competition (shared II. place). Social housing, urban planning, sustainable design and participatory architecture are his main research interests. He likes creating maquettes, wood furniture and anything that involves physical touch with materials. In his Bachelor's thesis he researched about the social housing programs in the Middle-Ferencváros district of Budapest, an area that went through many gentrification phases, and investigated what role architecture plays in this process.
