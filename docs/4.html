KINETIC TUNNEL

How does a space behave when being observed? Do we have an impact on spaces? And if so, who does that affect, the observer or the physical world, or maybe both? This project invites people to experience an environment that changes simply by observing it. By taking steps, the structure moves, and new spaces emerge. Using nature as a tool, we build a relationship between the perceived space and the observer. This structure shapes a bridge on the narrowest section of Lake Balaton in Hungary. By walking through it, you experience the changing environment in response to your interaction. And from the outside, this movement can be observed from the ferry and trom the water, detting something that is happening. Made in collaboration with Anna Flóra Kiss.
